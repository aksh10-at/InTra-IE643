## Decision Rule

$$\tilde{w} = \begin{bmatrix} w_1 \\ w_2 \\ w_3 \\ \vdots \\ w_d \\ -\theta \end{bmatrix}, \quad \tilde{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_d \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\sum_{j=1}^{d} w_j x_j \ge 0 \; (\ge 0) \quad \text{decision rule}$$

$$\langle \vec{w}, \vec{x} \rangle - \theta \ge 0$$
$$\langle \vec{w}, \vec{x} \rangle - \theta < 0$$

$$\langle \tilde{w}, \tilde{x} \rangle \ge 0$$
$$\langle \tilde{w}, \tilde{x} \rangle < 0$$

$$\tilde{w} = \begin{bmatrix} w_1 \\ w_2 \\ \vdots \\ w_d \end{bmatrix}, \quad \tilde{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_d \end{bmatrix}$$

# Train a perceptron:

$$D = \{(x^t, y^t)\} \quad (\text{online learning}), \qquad t=1,2,3,\dots$$

$\to \vec{w}^0 = \vec{0}$

$\to t=0,1,2,3,\dots$

$\to$ receive a sample $(x^t,y^t) \in D$

$\to \hat{x}^t = \begin{bmatrix} x^t \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

$\to \hat{y}^t = \text{Perceptron}(\hat{x}^t, \vec{w}^t)$ [Prediction]

**Update rule:**

if $\hat{y}^t == y^t$: no update, $\vec{w}^{t+1} = \vec{w}^t$
else: $\vec{w}^{t+1} = \vec{w}^t + y^t\hat{x}^t$

<!-- page: ie643_lec03_page006 -->

$$x_1 + x_2 = 4$$

$$\vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \langle \begin{bmatrix} w_1 \\ w_2 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} \rangle = 4 = 0$$

$$\text{sign}(z) = +1, z \ge 0$$
$$= -1, \text{otherwise}$$

$$\tilde{w} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ -4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad (x,y) \to ((2,1),+1)$$

$$\tilde{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\tilde{w} = \tilde{w} + y \tilde{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -4 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\text{Perceptron}(\tilde{x}) = \text{sign}(\langle \tilde{w}, \tilde{x} \rangle) = -1$$

# Hyperplane:

A set of points $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ for which there exist a non-zero $w \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and a scalar $b \in \mathbb{R}$ s.t. $\langle w, x \rangle = b$ holds.

### Halfspaces

$\langle w, x \rangle - b \ge 0$
$\langle w, x \rangle - b < 0$

two halfspaces associated with $\langle w, x \rangle - b = 0$

# Linear separability assumption:

$$D = \{(x^t, y^t)\}_{t=1,2,\dots} \quad (x^t \in \mathbb{R}^d, y^t \in \{+1,-1\})$$

is called linearly separable if there exists a non-zero $\underline{w^*} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and $\gamma > 0$ such that:

$$y^t \le w^*, x^t \ge \gamma \implies \langle w^*, x^t \rangle > \gamma \text{ if } y^t = +1$$
$$\langle w^*, x^t \rangle < -\gamma \text{ if } y^t = -1$$

<!-- page: ie643_lec03_page012 -->

# Convergence of perceptron happens under linear separability assumption

no weight update after finite number of mistakes made by the perceptron

$\to$ To run for a very large number of rounds — $T$
$\to$ The perceptron makes $M$ mistakes in these $T$ rounds
$\to$ To show: $M < \infty$, $M \le B$

<!-- page: ie643_lec03_page013 -->

Consider a round $t$ at which perceptron makes a mistake.

$$\hat{y}^t \ne y^t$$

$$w^{t+1} = w^t + y^t x^t \quad \text{before mistake}$$

$$\langle w^*, w^{t+1} \rangle \text{ and } \langle w^*, w^t \rangle \text{ behave?}$$

$$\langle w^{t+1}, w^* \rangle = \langle w + y^t x^t, w^* \rangle = \langle w, w^* \rangle + \langle y^t x^t, w^* \rangle = \langle w, w^* \rangle + y^t \langle x^t, w^* \rangle$$

<!-- page: ie643_lec03_page014 -->

$\vec{u}, \vec{v}, \vec{z}$.

$$\langle k\vec{u}+\beta\vec{v}, \vec{z}\rangle = k\langle\vec{u},\vec{z}\rangle + \beta\langle\vec{v},\vec{z}\rangle$$

$$\langle w^{t+1}, w^*\rangle - \langle w^t, w^*\rangle$$

$$= y^t \langle x^t, w^*\rangle > \gamma$$

$$\underbrace{\langle w^{t+1}, w^*\rangle - \langle w^t, w^*\rangle}_{> \gamma}$$

<!-- page: ie643_lec03_page015 -->

$$\sum_{t=0}^{T} \langle w^{t+1}, w^* \rangle - \langle w^t, w^* \rangle$$
