## Discrete Prob Distn

$$y = \begin{bmatrix} p_1 \\ p_2 \\ p_3 \\ \vdots \\ p_C \end{bmatrix}, \quad \{p_j \ge 0, \; \sum_j p_j = 1\}$$

$\downarrow$

$\{p_1, p_2, \dots, p_C\}$ which form a discrete prob. distn.

where the entire mass is on the $k^{th}$ component.

<!-- page: ie643_lec09_page008 -->

$$\hat{y} = \begin{bmatrix} q_1 \\ q_2 \\ \vdots \\ q_C \end{bmatrix}, \quad q_j \ge 0, \; \sum q_j = 1$$

$$\text{kL div b/w } p \text{ and } q$$
$$\text{kL}(p||q) = \sum_{j=1}^{C} p_j \log \frac{p_j}{q_j}$$

$$= E_{p} \log \frac{p}{q}$$

**Compare two discrete prob distri**

- KL divergence
- Bregman div
- JS div ...

$$\text{KL}(p \to q)$$

$$\text{KL}(q \to p) = \text{KL}(q \| p) = \sum_{j=1}^{C} q_j \log \frac{q_j}{p_j}$$

$$\text{KL}(p \| q) = \sum_{j=1}^{C} p_j \log p_j - \sum_{j=1}^{C} p_j \log q_j$$

$\to$ negative entropy.

$$\gamma = q \; \text{KL} \downarrow 0 \; \text{KL} \uparrow$$

## Logistic Regression

$$c(y^i,\hat{y}^i) = \text{KL}(y^i,\hat{y}^i)$$

$\to$ KL = 0 when $y^i = \hat{y}^i$, KL > 0 when $y^i \ne \hat{y}^i$

**logistic regression**

$$\min_{\text{weights of NN}} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{KL}(y^i,\hat{y}^i)$$

$$\min_{\text{weights}} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{CE}(y^i,\hat{y}^i)$$

$$E[y|x=\vec{x}] \to \text{Bernoulli r.v. likelihood}$$

<!-- page: ie643_lec09_page012 -->

$$\min_{\text{weights}} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left(-\sum_{j=1}^{C} y_j^i \log \hat{y}_j^i\right)$$

$$\min_{\text{weights}} \sum_{j=1}^{C} \hat{y}_j \log \frac{\hat{y}_j}{y_j} = \sum_{j=1}^{C} \hat{y}_j \log \hat{y}_j - \sum_{j=1}^{C} \hat{y}_j \log y_j$$
