## Descent Direction

A non-zero direction \( u \in \mathbb{R}^d \) is called a descent direction of \( f \) at \( x \) if:

$$
f'(x; u) < 0
$$

### Diagram: Contour Plot

The diagram on the right shows a contour plot with labeled axes \( w_1 \) and \( w_2 \). The contours represent the level sets of the function \( f \). The point marked by an "X" indicates the current position \( x \).

The arrows indicate the direction of the gradient vector \( \nabla f(x) \), which points towards the steepest ascent. A descent direction would be any direction that points away from the maximum value of the function.
