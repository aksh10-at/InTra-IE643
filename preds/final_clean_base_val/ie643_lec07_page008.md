# Negative Gradient Direction

The negative gradient direction is used in optimization algorithms to find the minimum of a function.

## Step-Length / Learning Rate

The step-length or learning rate determines how far along the negative gradient direction we move at each iteration.

### Mathematical Formulation

The derivative of a function \( f \) at point \( x \) in the direction \( y \) can be defined as:

$$
f'(x, y) = \lim_{\alpha \to 0} \frac{f(x + \alpha y) - f(x)}{\alpha}
$$

This formula represents the rate of change of the function \( f \) in the direction \( y \).
