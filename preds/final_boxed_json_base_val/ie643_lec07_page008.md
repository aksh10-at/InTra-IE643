# Negative Gradient Direction

The negative gradient direction is given by:

$$
-\nabla f(x)
$$

## Step-Length / Learning Rate

The step-length or learning rate is crucial in optimization algorithms.

### Derivative Definition

The derivative of a function \( f \) at point \( x \) in the direction \( y \) is defined as:

$$
f'(x, y) = \lim_{\alpha \to 0} \frac{f(x + \alpha y) - f(x)}{\alpha}
$$
