## Property:

If $u$ (a non-zero vector) is a descent direction of fn $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ at $x$, then:

there exists some $\epsilon \geq 0$ such that for every $\alpha \in [0,\epsilon]$

$$\lim_{\alpha\downarrow0}\frac{f(x+\alpha u)-f(x)}{\alpha}<0$$

$f(x+\alpha u)<f(x)$

$$\Rightarrow f'(x;u)<0$$
