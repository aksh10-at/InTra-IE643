The diagram depicts three clusters (Cluster 1, Cluster 2, and Cluster 3) with their respective regions marked by different colors:

Each cluster region is defined as the intersection of half-spaces. Specifically:

- **Cluster 1 Region**: Defined by the intersection of half-spaces around $\mu_1$.

### Key Points

1. **Distance Comparison**:
   - Points within a cluster will be closest to the centroid ($\mu_i$) of that cluster compared to any other centroid ($\mu_j$).

2. **Intersection of Half-Spaces**:
   - The regions of each cluster are formed by the intersections of half-spaces.
   - For $k$ clusters, there are $(k-1)$ intersections defining these regions.

3. **Voronoi Regions**:
   - The regions shown in the diagram are Voronoi regions, which are the areas closest to a specific centroid among all centroids.

4. **Mathematical Notation**:
   - $\mu_i$: Centroid of Cluster $i$
   - Half-spaces: Define the boundaries of the regions.

This diagram effectively visualizes how clusters are formed based on proximity to centroids and how these regions are defined through the intersection of half-spaces.
