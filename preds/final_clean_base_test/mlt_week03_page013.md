## Diagram: Objective Function and Penalty

The diagram illustrates the relationship between the objective function and penalty terms as a function of parameter k.

- **Objective Function**: Represented by the red line.
- **Penalty Function**: Represented by the blue line.
- **Penalty(k)**: The penalty term increases with k.
- **Intersection Point (k*)**: The point where the objective function and penalty function intersect.

## Some Common Criteria for Choosing [Obj + Penalty]

### AIC - Akaike Information Criterion
$$
\text{AIC} = 2k - 2 \log(L(\theta^*))
$$

### BIC - Bayesian Information Criterion
$$
\text{BIC} = k (\log n) - 2 \log(L(\theta^*))
$$
