Some common criterion for choosing $\boxed{\text{Obj} + \text{Penalty}}$

- **AIC** — Akaike Information Criterion: $\left[2k - 2\log(L(\theta^*))\right]$
- **BIC** — Bayesian Information Criterion: $\left[k(\log n) - 2\log(L(\theta^*))\right]$
