$$\tilde{w} = \begin{bmatrix} w_1 \\ w_2 \\ w_3 \\ \vdots \\ w_d \\ -\theta \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \tilde{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_d \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\sum_{j=1}^{d} w_j x_j \geqslant \theta \quad (\text{decision rule})$$

$$\langle \vec{w}, \vec{x} \rangle - \theta \geqslant 0$$
$$\langle \vec{w}, \vec{x} \rangle - \theta < 0$$

$$\langle \tilde{w}, \tilde{x} \rangle \geqslant 0$$
$$\langle \tilde{w}, \tilde{x} \rangle < 0$$

$$\vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix} w_1 \\ \vdots \\ w_d \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_d \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\frac{d+1}{\|w\|} = \frac{d+1}{\|\vec{x}\|}$$
