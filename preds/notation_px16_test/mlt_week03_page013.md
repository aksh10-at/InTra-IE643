Some common criterion for choosing $\boxed{\text{Obj}+\text{Penalty}}$

- AIC — Akaike Information Criterion: $[2K - 2\log(L(\theta^*))]$
- BIC — Bayesian Information Criterion: $[K(\log n) - 2\log(L(\theta^*))]$
