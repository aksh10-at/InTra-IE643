Projection of M on $(x,y)$ space is convex hull of R1 U R2

$$M_{convex} = \left\{ x^1, y^1, x^2, y^2; \quad x,y,\lambda^1,\lambda^2 \right\} \to \text{all are convex now}$$

$$\lambda^1g_1(\frac{x^1}{\lambda^1},\frac{y^1}{\lambda^1})\le0, \qquad \lambda^2g_1(\frac{x^2}{\lambda^2},\frac{y^2}{\lambda^2})\le0, \qquad \lambda^1+\lambda^2=1, \qquad y\ge0$$

$$x=\frac{x^1}{\lambda^1}+\frac{x^2}{\lambda^2}, \qquad y=\frac{y^1}{\lambda^1}+\frac{y^2}{\lambda^2}$$

**Perspective Function**
$$P(\cdot) = \begin{cases} \lambda^1g_1(\frac{x^1}{\lambda^1},\frac{y^1}{\lambda^1}) & \lambda^1>0 \\ 0 & \lambda^1=0 \end{cases}$$
