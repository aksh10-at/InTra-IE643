Some common criterion for choosing $\boxed{\text{Obj}+\text{penalty}}$

- **AIC** — Akaike Information Criterion
  $$2k - 2\log(L(o^*))$$

- **BIC** — Bayesian Information Criterion
  $$K(\log n) - 2\log(L(o^*))$$
