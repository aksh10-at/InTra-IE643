Projection of M on $(x,y)$ space is convex hull of RI $\cup R_2$

$$M_{convex} = \left\{ \bar{x}^1,\bar{y}^1,\bar{x}^2,\bar{y}^2; x,y,\lambda^1,\lambda^2 \right\}$$

$$\lambda^1g_1(\frac{\bar{x}^1}{x^1},\frac{\bar{y}^1}{x^1})\leq0$$
$$\lambda^2g_1(\frac{\bar{x}^2}{x^2},\frac{\bar{y}^2}{x^2})\leq0$$
$$\lambda^1+\lambda^2=1, \quad y\geq0$$

$$x=\frac{\bar{x}^1}{x^1}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{x^2}, \quad y=\frac{\bar{y}^1}{x^1}+\frac{\bar{y}^2}{x^2}$$

**perspective function**
$$p(\cdot) = \begin{cases} \lambda^1g_1\left(\frac{\bar{x}^1}{x^1},\frac{\bar{y}^1}{x^1}\right) & \lambda^1>0 \\ 0 & \lambda^1=0 \end{cases}$$
