# Diagram: Objective Function and Penalty

The graph shows the relationship between the objective function and penalty terms.

- **Objective Function**: The blue line represents the objective function.
- **Penalty Function**: The purple line represents the penalty function.
- **Intersection Point**: The point where the objective function and penalty function intersect is marked as \( k^* \).

## Common Criteria for Choosing \( Obj + Penalty \)

### AIC - Akaike Information Criterion
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} 2k_i - 2\log(L(\theta^*))
$$

### BIC - Bayesian Information Criterion
$$
K(\log n) - 2\log(L(\theta^*))
$$
