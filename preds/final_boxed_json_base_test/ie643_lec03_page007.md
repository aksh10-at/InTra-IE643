### Diagram: Online Learning Flowchart
- **t = 0**
  - **Input:** \( w^t \)
  - **Output:** \( y^t \)
  - **Update:** \( (x^t, y^t) \)

- **t+1**
  - **Input:** \( w^{t+1} \)
