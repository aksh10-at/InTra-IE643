**Directional derivative of** $f$ **at a point** $x$

$$f: \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}$$

along a non-zero direction $u$ is

$$f'(x; u) = \lim_{\alpha \downarrow 0} \frac{f(x + \alpha u) - f(x)}{\alpha}$$

[Diagram: A point $x$ with an arrow to $x + \alpha u$, and a further arrow continuing in direction $u$.]

$$f'(x; u) = \langle \text{grad of } f \text{ at } u, u \rangle$$

$u$: negative gradient direction
