## Superposn of sigmoids

$$f: [0,1]^d \to \mathbb{R}$$

Any continuous fn $f: [0,1]^d \to \mathbb{R}$ can be approximated by such a NN (with app. $\vec{w}_j, b_j, \alpha_j$) to any arbitrary accuracy.

$$|f(x) - NN(x)| < \epsilon \quad \forall x \in [0,1]^d$$

$$\epsilon \to 0 \; \Rightarrow \; N \uparrow$$
