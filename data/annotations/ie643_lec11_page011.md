A function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is called convex if:

$$f(z) \leq \lambda f(x_1) + (1-\lambda) f(x_2)$$

$z$ is on line seg connecting $x_1, x_2$: &nbsp; $z = \lambda x_1 + (1-\lambda) x_2$

$f$ defined over cvx sets.
