$$\nabla_x^2 f = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1 \partial x_1} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1 \partial x_n} \\ \vdots & & \vdots \\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n \partial x_1} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n \partial x_n} \end{bmatrix}$$

A matrix $A$ ($n \times n$) is positive semi-def if: $v^TAv \geq 0 \; \forall v \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $\langle v, Av \rangle \geq 0$

Hessian of $f$ is p.s.d $\iff$ the eigenvalues of $\nabla^2 f$ are non-negative.
