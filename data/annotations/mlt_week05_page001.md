# Supervised Learning

Input: $\{x_1,\dots,x_n\}$, $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^d \leftarrow$ Features
$\{y_1,\dots,y_n\} \leftarrow$ Labels

- $\{+1,-1\}$ or $\{0,1\}$ — **Binary Classification** (spam vs non spam)
- $\{0,1,\dots,k\}$ — **Multi Classification** (digit classification)
- $\in \mathbb{R}$ — **Regression** (rainfall prediction)
