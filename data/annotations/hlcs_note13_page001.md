# C++ Tutorials (13)

**Q ⇒** What do you mean by operators in C++?

**Sol ⇒** An operator is a symbol that tells the compiler to perform some specific mathematical or logical manipulations.

C++ provide the following types of operators:

[Diagram: Box "Operators" branching into Arithmetic operators, Relational operators, Logical operators, Assignment operators, Bitwise operators, and (below Arithmetic/Relational/Logical) Conditional operators, Increment/decrement operator.]

**1) Arithmetic Operators ⇒** These operators are used to perform arithmetic/mathematical operations on operands.

Ex ⇒ `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`

```
   6
2)13     13/2 = 6
  12
  --
   1      13%2 = 1
```
(`/` → quotient, `%` → reminder)
