## Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

$$|\langle \vec{u}, \vec{v} \rangle| \leq \|\vec{u}\|_2 \|\vec{v}\|_2$$

$$\vec{u} = \begin{bmatrix} u_1 \\ u_2 \\ \vdots \\ u_d \end{bmatrix}$$

$\rightarrow L_2$ norm or Euclidean length of $\vec{u}$.

$$\|u\|_2 = \sqrt{u_1^2 + u_2^2 + \cdots + u_d^2}$$

$\rightarrow$ dist$(\vec{u}, \vec{0})$
