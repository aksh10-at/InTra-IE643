However this might not be the correct value of $p$. Could be anything from 0.00001 to 2/3 but **not** 0 or 1.

We have some assumptions here:

The observations are —
(I) independent $\left[P(X_i/X_j) = P(X_i) \; \forall i\neq j\right]$

(II) identically distributed $\left[P(X_i=1) = P(X_j=1) = p \; \forall i,j\right]$
