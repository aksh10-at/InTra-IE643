## Convex function:

A function $f: C \to \mathbb{R}$ over a convex set $C$ is called convex if:

for any $x_1, x_2 \in C$ and for $\lambda \in [0,1]$

$$f(\lambda x_1 + (1-\lambda)x_2) \leq \lambda f(x_1) + (1-\lambda)f(x_2)$$
