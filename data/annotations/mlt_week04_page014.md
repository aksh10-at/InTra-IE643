For concave,

$$f(\lambda_1a_1+\lambda_2a_2+\cdots+\lambda_ka_k) \geq \lambda_1f(a_1)+\cdots+\lambda_kf(a_k)$$

$$\therefore \; \boxed{f\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}\lambda_ka_k\right) \geq \sum_{i=1}^{k}\lambda_if(a_i)}$$

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_i=1, \quad \lambda_i \in [0,1]$$
