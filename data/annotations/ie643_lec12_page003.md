## Convexity:

**Convex combination of 2 pts.**

[Diagram: A line segment with points $x_1, x_2$ on it (convex combination); a non-convex triangle-like zigzag shape for comparison.]

$$\lambda x_1 + (1-\lambda) x_2, \quad \lambda \in [0,1]$$

A set $C$ is convex if for any arbitrary pair $x_1, x_2 \in C$, the cvx combination $\lambda x_1 + (1-\lambda)x_2$ also belongs to $C$.
