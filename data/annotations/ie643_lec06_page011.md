## Mathematical Optimization

**0-1**, **sigmoid**

[Diagram: Graph of $\sigma(z)$ — an S-shaped curve rising from 0 to 1, crossing at $(0, 0.5)$ roughly, with asymptotes at $y=0$ and $y=1$.]

$$\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(-z)}$$

$$\lim_{z \to \infty} \sigma(z) \to 1, \qquad \lim_{z \to -\infty} \sigma(z) \to 0$$
